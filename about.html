The Materials

Sourced Consciously, Woven Lovingly

Mulberry Silk

100% organic mulberry silk harvested in sustainable silkworm farms. Extracted using traditional zero-chemical processes for a fluid, weightless drape that flows like liquid.

Italian Flax Linen

Locally cultivated in the fields of Tuscany. Naturally irrigated by rainfall and woven into exceptionally breathable, durable fabrics that soften beautifully with age.

Australian Merino

Extra-fine Merino wool sourced from certified non-mulesed farms. Offers natural temperature regulation, outstanding breathability, and a incredibly soft next-to-skin touch.

Mongolian Cashmere

Grade-A fibers combed gently from free-roaming cashmere goats. Responsibly harvested to preserve grassland ecosystems, providing unparalleled, cloud-like softness.

Our Method

The Artisanal Journey

01

Bespoke Architectural Sketching

Every capsule starts with hand-drawn structural sketches at our Saint-HonorÃ© atelier, drawing silhouettes inspired by neoclassical geometry and structural fluidity.

02

Zero-Waste Pattern Engineering

Our designers arrange pattern layouts with extreme precision to minimize fabric scraps, utilizing heritage cutting methodologies that achieve up to 98% material yield.

03

Hand-Stitched Tailoring

Experienced tailors spend upwards of 18 hours hand-crafting each blazer and robe. Fine details such as pick stitching, bound seams, and shell buttons are sewn completely by hand.
