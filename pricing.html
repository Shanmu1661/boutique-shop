Common Inquiries

Styling Consultations FAQ

Can styling sessions be conducted virtually?

Yes, we offer fully digital consultations for international clients. You will receive step-by-step guidance on self-measurement and visual reviews via secure high-definition video calls.

What should I prepare for a Wardrobe Makeover?

Before your stylist arrives, we recommend preparing all current outfits in a single area. Do not worry about folding or sorting; our stylist will catalog and assess every item to build your new tailored capsule profile.

Are the tailoring costs included in the packages?

For Couture Club VIP members, tailoring alterations on all Avenoir brand purchases are completely unlimited and free. For hourly styling sessions or wardrobe makeovers, basic alterations can be added at standard studio rates.
