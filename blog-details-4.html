The Geometry of Tailoring: Behind the Neoclassical Silhouettes

Marc-Antoine Valois Creative Director

Tailoring is more than a process of joining textiles; it is an exercise in structural geometry. At House of Avenoir, our pattern engineering relies on the principles of architectural line and gravity, ensuring that every blazer and drape flows smoothly with the natural movement of the body.

Neoclassical design centers on proportion. When drafting a sleeve, we consider the balance of the shoulder line, the sweep of the armscye, and the canvas layering. By employing ancient hair-canvas stitching alongside modern laser layouts, we create jackets that hold their form without rigidity.

"A pattern is a series of mathematical equations balanced on the shoulders. If the shoulder anchors correctly, the rest of the fabric falls in absolute harmony."

Our commitment to zero-waste cutting also influences this geometric layout. By aligning curves interlocking like puzzle pieces, we minimize gaps and waste, transforming luxury tailoring into a completely sustainable, zero-waste art form.
